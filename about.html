Company Overview

Mandoxy MEP Engineers is a professional consulting firm specializing in Mechanical, Electrical, Plumbing (MEP), and Building Management Systems (BMS) engineering services.

We design safe, efficient, and cost-effective building services systems for residential, commercial, industrial, and hospitality projects. Our approach integrates modern engineering practices, Building Information Modelling (BIM), and energy-efficient strategies to deliver world-class solutions.

  • Full MEP design documentation
  • BMS integration and controls
  • Energy modeling and optimization
  • Site supervision and commissioning

Mission: To deliver reliable, compliant, and efficient MEP engineering designs that enhance building performance, improve comfort and safety, and meet the highest industry standards.

Capabilities & Growth Plans

Founded in 2025, Mandoxy is building its project record while offering professional MEP, BMS, BIM/Revit, supervision, and commissioning support.

Core Capabilities

  • MEP design support for commercial, residential, hospitality, industrial, and healthcare sectors.
  • BMS integration and controls planning for smart-building operations.
  • Energy-efficient HVAC design strategies to support lower operating costs.
  • Documentation aligned with professional engineering standards.
  • Practical BIM/Revit coordination, tendering, supervision, and commissioning support.

Future Plans

  • Establish regional offices in Southern Africa, including South Africa, Zambia, and Botswana.
  • Develop smart-building solutions integrating IoT and AI for building automation.
  • Launch training programs for engineers on BMS, HVAC, and energy efficiency.
  • Build a transparent project record through pilot commercial and industrial work.
  • Invest in sustainable, energy-efficient engineering solutions.
